Questions, answered.

Is Otto really free?

Yes. Otto is free to download and use, with no account and no paywall. If it earns a place on your Mac, you can buy me a coffee. The app stays free either way.

Does my Mac need a notch?

Otto is designed around the notch, but you can choose a floating panel in setup or in Settings. It opens with a shortcut instead. Otto needs macOS 13 or later.

Which calendars does it read?

The ones in your Mac’s Calendar app, including iCloud, Google and Exchange accounts you’ve added there. Otto only reads events. It never creates, edits or deletes one.

Where is my data?

On your Mac. To-dos and notes are stored locally, and a Markdown copy lives in a folder you choose (by default Documents/Otto). There is no Otto account and nothing syncs through a server.

What does the anonymous usage data include?

Only if you say yes during setup: which features are used, the app and macOS version, and a random ID that isn’t linked to you. Never your to-dos, notes, calendar or anything you type. You can see exactly what is sent in Settings. Full details.

Will macOS warn me when I open it?

No. Otto is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app. It also updates itself from inside the app.

Which languages does it speak?

English and Italian.
